# Asset Provenance

This is a pointer, not the inventory. The current, maintained asset
provenance inventory lives under `docs/release/`:

- `../release/ASSET_PROVENANCE.md` - per-asset source, licence, and disclosure
  route for the current release.
- `../release/THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.json`
- `../release/LICENSE_AUDIT_REPORT.md`
- `../release/LICENSE_RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md`

Re-run that inventory after any asset replacement or newly imported/bundled
material, and keep `PUBLIC_RELEASE_READINESS_TRACKER.md` and
`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` synchronized with it.
