## What Inter Galactic Is

Inter Galactic is a Matrix client. It is software you use to connect to a Matrix homeserver. Inter Galactic should be treated as separate from any private homeserver or friend-group domain unless a public release specifically says otherwise. Your Matrix account and server-side data are controlled by the homeserver you choose.

Native mobile builds may not create Matrix accounts. You may need an existing Matrix account from `matrix.org`, a self-hosted homeserver, or another provider.

## Your Responsibilities

You are responsible for:

- choosing a homeserver you trust;
- keeping your account credentials, recovery key, and devices secure;
- following the selected homeserver's terms and policies;
- following the rules of rooms and communities you join;
- using third-party integrations only as permitted by those providers.
