# Inter Galactic Community Guidelines

Publication status: draft pending operator review for any public Inter Galactic
support space. These guidelines describe the intended moderation baseline, not
an already-launched public service commitment.

These guidelines describe recommended behavior for any public Inter Galactic support space if one exists. They do not replace the rules of the Matrix homeserver or room you choose.

## Be Safe And Respectful

Do not harass, bully, stalk, threaten, dox, or encourage harm against another person or group.

Do not post or request illegal content, exploitative content, malware, stolen credentials, or instructions intended to cause real-world harm.

Do not share sexual content involving minors. Do not share non-consensual sexual content or intimate images. Do not pressure people for sexual content.

Do not impersonate people, organizations, moderators, homeserver operators, Inter Galactic, Matrix.org, or Commet.

Do not spam, mass-invite, coordinate raids, evade blocks or bans, or abuse notification, calling, upload, or reporting tools.

Do not post another person's private information without permission.

## Moderation Expectations

Room moderators and homeserver administrators control their own Matrix rooms and servers. Inter Galactic can assist with app-level reports and any public support space it actually operates, but it cannot moderate every room or homeserver on the Matrix network.

Use the in-app Report action where available. Inter Galactic should submit message, room, and user reports through the selected homeserver's Matrix-native reporting pathway. You can also block or ignore abusive users through Matrix ignored-user controls. Direct Inter Galactic contact is for app-level issues, public Inter Galactic support spaces if any exist, unavailable Matrix report flows, or routing help.

Reports should include the Matrix user ID, room ID or alias, event link or event ID, homeserver, date/time, and a short explanation. Inter Galactic contact: `intergalactic@ourgalaxy.space`. Do not forward illegal sexual content involving minors; report it through the proper emergency and legal channels.

For the reporting path details and current release-readiness caveats, see
`REPORT_ABUSE.md`.

## Emergencies

Inter Galactic is not an emergency service. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.
