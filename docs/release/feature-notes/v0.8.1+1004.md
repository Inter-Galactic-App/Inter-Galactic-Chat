# Inter Galactic v0.8.1

Build: v0.8.1+1004

## Soundboard Packs

Soundboard packs are here. Organize your sounds into packs and use them across
spaces.

## Emoticons

- Reorder emoticons in the picker and within their packs.
- The Emoticon Creator has a refreshed experience. Give it a try.

## Mobile

- Notifications now support rich previews, replies, and mute actions.
- Share content from other apps directly to Inter Galactic, and paste images
  into messages.

## Inbox

The new Inbox makes it easy to see recent messages, jump to their rooms, or
mark everything as read.

## Messaging

- Mention search now matches usernames, nicknames, and account names.
- Pinned messages in encrypted rooms are decrypted when opened from the pins
  view.

## Voice Rooms

Voice-room chat is more stable, but it remains a work in progress.
