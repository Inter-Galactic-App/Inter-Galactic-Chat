# Stories

Photo stories are now available from Home. Tap your story bubble to add a
photo, decorate it, and share it with people you already message.

## How To Try It

- Open Home and choose your story bubble to add a photo.
- Add text, emoji, stickers, or mentions before sharing.
- Tap a friend's story bubble to view and react.
- Stories expire automatically after 24 hours.
- Replies and reactions stay connected to the existing direct message.

## Availability

You will only see stories from people who already have a direct message with
you.
