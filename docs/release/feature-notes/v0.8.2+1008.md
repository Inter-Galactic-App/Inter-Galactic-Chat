# Inter Galactic v0.8.2

Build: v0.8.2+1008

## Link Previews

For supported TikTok, Instagram, and Reddit links, the app tries the configured
preview service and homeserver first. If they cannot build a full card, direct
fallback can ask the link site. The URL may reach each service in that chain;
the site sees that your device requested it. It is off by default, with
separate switches for encrypted and unencrypted chats.

## Voice

On Windows, Hush filters nearby talk Enhanced suppression misses, but uses
extra CPU and may make your mic choppy on a busy PC. The optional Loud-Speech
Guard stability setting can reduce flutter or pops from repeated protection
switching; it is off by default.

## Messaging And Stickers

Whisper gives messages smaller text. Recent sticker picks collect in a
device-local Frequently Used pack.

## Activity And Help

Spotify reconnect needs and Steam issues appear in Alerts with a shortcut to
Activity settings. The FAQ has fresh help for Stories, Soundboard,
Desktop Companion, activity privacy, noise suppression, effects, themes, and
accessibility.
