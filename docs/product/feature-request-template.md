## Product Triage

**Status:**
- `investigating` - The need is real enough to research, but the design,
  technical approach, or priority is not settled.
- `planned` - The team intends to build it and has a clear direction.
- `rejected` - The request is not a fit, is too risky, conflicts with product
  direction, or is better handled outside Inter Galactic.
- `completed` - The request shipped or the user-visible need is otherwise met.
